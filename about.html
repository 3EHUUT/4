About Summit Force

Hey there, fellow adventurer. We're Summit Force — a small team of hikers, climbers, and camp-obsessed gear nerds who spend way too much time testing equipment in the backcountry so you don't have to waste money on stuff that fails when it matters most.

We started this site in 2026 with a simple idea: finding great outdoor gear shouldn't require a PhD in product research. There are thousands of options out there, and honestly, most of them are fine. But "fine" isn't what you want when you're 30 miles from the trailhead in freezing rain.

What We Do

Our editorial team brings decades of combined experience across hiking, mountaineering, camping, trail running, and backcountry skiing. We personally test products, dig into specs, compare prices across retailers, and curate only the gear we'd actually trust on our own trips.

  • Honest, hands-on reviews from people who actually use the gear
  • Curated picks at fair prices — no inflated recommendations
  • Guides written for real-world conditions, not showroom floors

How We Keep the Lights On

We earn a small commission through affiliate links, which means when you buy a product we recommend, we get a small percentage at no extra cost to you. This is what allows us to keep doing what we love — testing gear, writing honest reviews, and helping you get outside with confidence.

We never let commissions influence our recommendations. If something isn't worth your money, we'll tell you straight.

Got questions or gear requests? Drop us a line. We read everything.
