Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

Summit Force ("we," "us," or "our") operates an e-commerce affiliate website focused on outdoor gear, apparel, and accessories. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our site.

Information We Collect

Usage Data

When you browse our site, we automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and click interactions
  • Referring website or search terms that led you to our site
  • Date and time of each visit

Device Information

We may collect information about the device you use to access our site, including:

  • Device type, operating system, and browser version
  • Screen resolution and language preferences
  • IP address (anonymized where required by law)

Newsletter Subscription

If you voluntarily choose to subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. This is entirely optional and requires your explicit consent. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link provided in every email we send.

Cookies

Our site uses cookies to ensure proper functionality and improve your experience. The types of cookies we use include:

  • Essential cookies — required for the site to function correctly, such as remembering your cookie preferences and maintaining session stability.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors interact with our site so we can improve content and navigation. These cookies collect aggregated, anonymous data.
  • Affiliate cookies — when you click a product link, a cookie may be placed by our affiliate partners to attribute the referral. These cookies are necessary for the affiliate program to function and do not collect personal information beyond the click and any resulting transaction.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling essential cookies may affect site functionality.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate and maintain the website
  • Analyze site traffic and usage patterns to improve our content
  • Send newsletters to subscribers who have opted in
  • Ensure proper attribution through our affiliate partnerships

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share anonymized, aggregated data with analytics providers and affiliate networks solely for the purposes described in this policy. These third-party services are bound by their own privacy policies and applicable data protection laws.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal data.
  • Opt-out — withdraw consent for newsletter communications at any time.
  • Portability — request your data in a structured, commonly used format.

For EEA/UK Residents (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, our legal bases for processing your data are legitimate interest (site analytics and improvement) and consent (newsletter subscription and non-essential cookies). You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

For California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request its deletion, and opt out of any sale of personal information. As stated above, we do not sell your personal data. To exercise your rights, contact us using the information below.

Data Retention

We retain usage and device data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. Newsletter subscriber email addresses are retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information collected through our site. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us through the contact information provided on our website.
